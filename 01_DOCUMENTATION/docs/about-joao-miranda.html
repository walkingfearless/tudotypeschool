About Your Instructor

João Miranda

João Miranda is a Portuguese multidisciplinary designer and type designer based in London. With over a decade of experience at some of the world’s most respected design consultancies — including Pentagram, Wolff Olins, R/GA, and DixonBaxi — he brings a rare blend of brand strategy, visual design, and deep typographic expertise to everything he does.

He currently works as Brand Designer at Automattic, shaping the visual identity of WordPress — the platform that powers over 40% of the web.

Career Highlights

João’s career spans branding, type design, and visual communication at global scale. Before joining Automattic, he worked as a Senior Visual Designer at PlayStation (via R/GA), where he led UI/UX improvements across marketing CRM systems and PlayStation.com — navigating complex organisational structures while elevating the craft and quality of design output at speed. He also built custom design-operations plugins to streamline workflows for his entire team.

At Pentagram London, he contributed to the rebrands of two AI companies. For Cohere, the team — led by partners Jody Hudson-Powell and Luke Powell — developed a visual identity rooted in the concept of “New Nature,” grounded in Voronoi diagrams and organic tessellations. João’s type expertise was central to the project: the rebrand delivered a comprehensive bespoke type system including Cohere Text, Cohere Outline, Cohere Mono, and a striking Headline variant featuring Voronoi cell cuts that animate as you type. For Tractable, an applied AI company that had recently achieved Unicorn status, Pentagram created a new symbol, wordmark, graphic system, and a bespoke “Damage Signature” tool for generating brand imagery — along with a custom Pantone ink mix named “Tractable Blue.”

At Wolff Olins, he worked on the Decathlon rebrand — a two-year transformation that unified a portfolio of 85 brands under one identity. The project included the creation of Decathlon Sans (with Grilli Type), a custom typeface whose slanted alternate characters inject movement and dynamism into the brand’s visual voice. João also worked on the AudioNova rebrand at Wolff Olins, where he was specifically brought in to explore how to visualise sound in compelling ways.

Earlier in his career, he spent time at BBC Creative and BBC Studios, and has also worked with clients including Facebook, Virgin, and WWF.

Type Design & tudotype

Beyond agency work, João is a dedicated type designer and the founder of tudotype, an independent type foundry. His typeface catalogue includes families such as BIG — used in Agua Castello Visual identity, Calma, Addfuel, Metrobank Sans, and he has designed custom typefaces for global brand projects, including the Cohere and Decathlon type systems.

His exhibition Big Kitchen, born from a question about type anatomy, was an interactive experience built around an exhaustive exploration of the BIG type family. Shown in Lisbon, Faro, and the Porto Design Biennale 2019, the exhibition drew a parallel between typeface design and cooking — and was recognised with an ADC Award.

Teaching & Knowledge Sharing

Knowledge sharing is fundamental to João’s practice. He has delivered talks and workshops at universities and industry events, including “Typography is Multidimensional” at the University of Aveiro, “Mistaker Maker” at First Round by UnderConsideration, and a keynote — “Tudotype: Where Type Comes First” — at designFAO 2022. He has also spoken at many Universities throughout Portugal.

This workshop at UAlg continues that commitment: bringing real-world type design experience into the classroom and giving students hands-on exposure to the craft.

Recognition

João’s work has been recognised with awards including an ADC Award (Merit for Typography), a Pentawards Gold Medal (Luxury Wine & Champagne Packaging), Indigo Awards (Gold and Silver), and inclusion in Creativepool’s Top 25 Typographers.
